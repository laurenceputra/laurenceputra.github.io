At TikTok, I work on reducing manual operational load and shortening triage. Project Stable Staples was a different setting: during COVID, I co-founded a volunteer response for families in rental communities. We raised over SGD 160,000, supported more than 600 households, built a donation portal in under two days, and operated with zero expense ratio. Both experiences required getting close to the work before choosing what to change.
