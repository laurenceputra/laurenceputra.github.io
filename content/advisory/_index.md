I help mission-driven teams understand where work stalls and what can be simplified.

This may be useful if decisions keep circling back, handoffs slow down delivery, or new AI tools add review work instead of removing it. I start with the work itself: who owns it, where it waits, and what a change would need to improve.

I work with nonprofit boards, social-impact organizations, and technical or leadership teams facing these problems. I am less useful for a standalone software build or a generic AI demo.
